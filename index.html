AI Shorts Generator

AI Shorts Generator is an independent desktop video-editing application. It helps users create short vertical clips from videos they choose.

Users review and edit each video caption and hashtags, select the available privacy setting, and explicitly confirm before sending a selected video to their own TikTok account.

Videos are processed by the application on the user's computer. Depending on the user's configuration, audio, transcripts, or text may be processed by a separately configured AI provider. A video is sent to TikTok only when the user confirms publication.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
