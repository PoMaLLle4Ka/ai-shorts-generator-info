Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy describes how AI Shorts Generator, an independent desktop video-editing application, handles information when you use it.

Information processed on your computer

Video files, generated clips, subtitles, and related job files are stored in the application's local storage on your computer. You control access to that computer and can remove these files using the application or by deleting them from its storage folder.

Optional AI providers

Depending on your configuration, audio, transcripts, or text may be sent to an AI provider that you have configured. Processing by that provider is governed by its own privacy policy and terms. The application does not send video or text to an AI provider unless the relevant feature is used and configured.

TikTok

If you connect a TikTok account, the application uses TikTok Login Kit to authorize access. Authorization tokens are stored on your computer and encrypted for your Windows user account. You can disconnect your account in the application.

A selected video and its caption, including hashtags, are sent to TikTok only after you review the publication settings and explicitly confirm publishing. TikTok then processes that information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Website hosting

This information page is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical information such as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The application is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the application or its integrations change. Questions about privacy can be sent to danil.romoshenko@gmail.com.
